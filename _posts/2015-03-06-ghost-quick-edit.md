I post new content to this blog every week. I do my best to guess what the formatting is going to look like in production, but 50% of the time, I get it wrong and I need to edit it.

####Quick Edit

To make a quick change to your post, type `/edit` after the post's URL.

So, if you're here:
![](/assets/images/2015/03/Screen-Shot-2015-03-05-at-4-51-34-PM.png)

write this:

![](/assets/images/2015/03/Screen-Shot-2015-03-05-at-4-52-35-PM.png)

and hit enter.

Edit as you normally would.
